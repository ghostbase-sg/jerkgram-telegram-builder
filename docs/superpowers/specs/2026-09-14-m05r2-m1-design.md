## M1 Settings foundation

`Jerkgram.dylib` owns all new product code. Feature semantics are split from Telegram 12.9.4 adapter glue:

- `Jerkgram/Settings/*`: schema, typed values, in-memory state, persistence, migration.
- `Jerkgram/Localization/*`: tweak-owned EN/RU strings with deterministic fallback.
- `Jerkgram/UI/*`: Jerkgram settings and About UI.
- `Jerkgram/Adapters/Telegram1294/*`: the narrow Settings-entry/runtime integration only.

Do not use old Telegram 12.9.2 source owners as hook addresses. Research the production 12.9.4 owner from the carrier and prefer an exported/ObjC/runtime-stable boundary. If no safe Settings entry boundary exists, M1 stops at owner research rather than stacking speculative hooks.

### Persistence contract

All account-scoped settings persist as:

`jerkgram.account.<accountPeerId>.setting.<baseKey>`

Global settings use their canonical global key directly. `jerkgram.global.DownloadBoost` is global. The store is typed/versioned, maintains an in-memory cache, performs controlled writes off render/layout paths, and never traverses the whole defaults domain during cell updates or scrolling.

Legacy migration is one-shot and case-insensitive from `GhostBase.*` and `GB.*` into canonical `jerkgram.*`. Canonical values win. Legacy values are copied, not deleted. Historical marker: `jerkgram.runtime.namespaceMigration.v1`.

## UI and localization

Add a native-looking Jerkgram entry in Telegram Settings. Opening it shows coherent sections for Ghost Mode, Messages, Protected Content, Stories/Media, Profile, Appearance, Other/Experimental where semantically appropriate, and About. In M1 all feature controls are UI + persistence only; they do not alter Telegram message/runtime behavior.

Tweak-owned strings ship in English and Russian. English is canonical fallback. The localization layer is owned by Jerkgram and does not depend directly on the old 12.9.2 `JerkgramStrings` plumbing.

About reports `Jerkgram 1.0.2` and base `Telegram 12.9.4`.
